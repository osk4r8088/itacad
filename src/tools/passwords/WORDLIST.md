# Word list

`eff_large_wordlist.txt` is the EFF Large Wordlist for Passphrases (7776 words, one per roll of five dice),
by the Electronic Frontier Foundation: https://www.eff.org/dice

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States (CC BY 3.0 US).
The site credits the EFF on the password page.

Copied unchanged from the npm package `eff-diceware-passphrase@3.0.0`; checked against
`diceware-wordlist-en-eff@1.0.1` (identical). `passwords.test.ts` checks length, rolls and uniqueness.
